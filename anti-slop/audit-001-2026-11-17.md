# antislop audit 001: Bento Coach UI

Date: 2026-11-17
Mode: after (session override)
Scope: `app/page.tsx`, `app/scan/page.tsx`, `app/layout.tsx`, `app/globals.css`, `components/*`
Skills: antislop (core), antislop-ui
Direction: `DESIGN.md`

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks and Liveliness = LOW.
Nothing below has been changed yet. Reply with the numbers you want fixed; numbers you don't name are left alone.

## Findings

### HIGH

1. **R-25: white text on the green buttons fails in dark mode.** White on `#5cbf85` is 2.27:1 (needs 4.5:1). Affects "Scan a meal", "Save to today", "Try again", "Retake" (no-food screen) and the numbered circles in the coach card. Fix: add an `on-accent` text token, white in light mode and `#0f1a14` in dark mode (7.85:1).
2. **R-39: no control shows a pointer cursor.** Tailwind v4 gives buttons `cursor: default`, and no `cursor-pointer` is set anywhere. Disabled controls (Save, portion stepper) don't show `not-allowed` either. Fix: one base rule in `globals.css` for buttons, links, labels and disabled states.
3. **R-39: most controls have no hover or pressed state.** These have none: Goal button, goal presets, Retake, Save, Try again, Upload photo, Back. "Scan a meal" has press only. Fix: a consistent hover (darker fill or stronger border) and press state on every control.
4. **R-32 / R-39: the dish-name field removes its focus outline and has no visible label.** It uses `outline-none` and replaces the outline with a 1px border color change, which is easy to miss. "Dish name" is screen-reader only. None of the other controls define a focus style either; they rely on the browser default. Fix: a visible `focus-visible` ring used everywhere, and a small visible label above the field while renaming.
5. **R-27: Today briefly shows the wrong state on load.** Before saved meals load from the browser, the page renders "Nothing logged yet" and a full "2100 kcal left" ring, then jumps to the real numbers. Fix: show a neutral loading state until the data is read.

### MEDIUM

6. **R-04 (Emoji as Decoration): 📷 on "Scan a meal" and 🍱 on the Coach label.** antislop treats emoji in UI text as a generated-UI tell. `DESIGN.md` currently allows these two, so under R-37 this is your call: keep them (I'll note the override) or drop them (text only, or a real icon).
7. **R-06: uppercase, wide-tracked labels.** "COACH" and "WHAT WE SEE · FIX NAMES, PORTIONS, OR REMOVE MISTAKES". The second is a full sentence in capitals and hard to read. Fix: sentence case, semibold. For example, a "What we see" heading with "Fix names, portions, or remove mistakes" as a muted hint below.
8. **R-06: Geist has no written reason.** It's the `create-next-app` default font, and nothing in `DESIGN.md` says why it fits a warm, local food app. This is a direction decision: keep Geist and write the reason down, or choose a typeface that matches the personality.

### LOW

9. **Liveliness: no dials or Design Read in `DESIGN.md`.** Proposed line: "Reading this as: a phone-first meal checker for people eating out in Taiwan, in a warm paper-and-food-colors language, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 2." RHYTHM 1 is deliberate (a tool, not a landing page). MOTION 2 covers the ring and bar fill transitions.
10. **R-20: no single focal number on the scan result.** kcal, protein, carbs and fat sit in four equal tiles, so nothing leads. Fix: make kcal the large number and put the three macros in a smaller row under it.
11. **R-21: theme reason not written down.** The app follows the phone's light/dark setting with no in-app toggle. That's a defensible choice for a phone tool, but `DESIGN.md` doesn't say so. Fix: write the reason, or add a toggle.
12. **Language of Chinese names.** Dish names such as 珍珠奶茶 render inside `<html lang="en">`, so screen readers read them with English pronunciation. Fix: `lang="zh-Hant"` on the local-name spans.
